Whitney stratifications of algebraic varieties and of polynomial maps, from their equations. A reimplementation, without Macaulay2, of Martin Helmer's WhitneyStratifications package, named in his memory, and many times faster.

About Helmer

A Whitney stratification breaks a singular variety into smooth pieces that fit together so regularly that its topology is locally constant along each piece: the starting point for computing the topology of singular spaces and the singularities of maps and mechanisms. Martin Helmer and I gave an algorithm for computing them from conormal spaces; Helmer computes them, and stratifications of polynomial maps, from equations with rational coefficients. It is a C++ library with Python bindings and a command-line program, built on msolve and FLINT.
